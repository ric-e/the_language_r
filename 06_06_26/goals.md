# Goals for Today

## Goal 1
`R`'s output model. This goal focuses on understanding the difference between `print` and `cat`, as well as `R`'s infamous auto-printing. It looks at the number prefixes like `[1]`, and explores the special text formatting `R` supports, like `paste`, `past0`, `sprintf`, and `format`

## Goal 2

Assignment and atomic-vectors. This includes `R`'s operators `<-`, `=`, `->`, and `<<-`. It looks at `R`'s weirdest idea, that there are no scalars, and numbers like `3` are 1-length vectors.

## Goal 3
Vecotrized thinking. Vectors are fundamental and incredibly important in `R`, mirroring how many other programming languages make good use of elements such as the `list` and `array`. Including `R`'s vector building with `c`, `:`, `seq`, and `rep`. `R` also explores elementwise arithmetic, the recycling rule, and `R`'s indexing rules.
